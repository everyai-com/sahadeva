# Prashna Astrology Corpus Study Map

This is Sahadeva's working interpretation layer for the available Prashna corpus. It does not replace the source books. Exact degrees, Sanskrit verses, charts, numerical combinations, medical statements, and remedies must be checked against the preserved originals.

## Do not merge the four systems blindly

The books use different engines of judgment. A conclusion must name the system being applied.

| School | Primary engine | Event promise | Timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Systems' Approach | Functional nature by ascendant, moolatrikona lordship, MEP/MMP, close conjunctions and aspects | Strength of the relevant house, lord, significator and operating period | Vimshottari main/sub-period interaction with transit contacts |
| Chappanna classical Prashna | Lagna, house lords, occupants, aspects, dignity, Navamsa and Drekkana | Benefic/malefic testimony and topic-specific stanzas | Sign modality, planetary motion, degrees and stanza-specific units |
| Prasna Tantra/Tajaka | Querent lord, event lord, Moon, dignity and Tajaka aspects | Applying connection or transfer of light among significators | Orb/distance, applying motion and movable/fixed/dual context |
| Prashna Nadi/KP | Seed-derived cusp, star lord, sub-lord, cuspal sub-lord and house significations | Repetition of favourable or obstructive house combinations | Dasa-Bhukti-Antara plus transits, with faster planets precipitating events |

## Common workflow found across the corpus

1. Establish a sincere, specific question. Casual testing is discouraged.
2. Fix the chart basis: question time and place, or the 1–249 seed procedure in Prashna Nadi.
3. Identify the querent from the ascendant and the matter from its relevant house.
4. If the question concerns another person, derive that person's ascendant and houses from the stated relationship.
5. Judge the ascendant, its lord, the event house and lord, the Moon, natural significators, occupants and relevant connections.
6. Test strength, affliction, obstruction and capacity using the selected school's definitions.
7. Separate promise from timing. A timing trigger cannot manufacture an event that the school considers unpromised.
8. Use multiple testimonies and resolve contradictions by strength; do not base a judgment on one isolated factor.
9. Validate the interpretation against the relevant case-study pattern.
10. Treat remedies as a separate layer, never as a substitute for chart judgment.

## Application of Prasna Astrology

### Foundations

- Defines signs, houses, planets and planetary periods before applying the Systems' Approach.
- Catalogues sign sex, rising style, reptile classification and directions.
- Distinguishes kendras, trikonas, dusthanas and benefic houses and defines the effective extent of a house.
- Uses planetary natural significations but gives decisive weight to functional lordship for the ascendant.

### Analytical engine

- Functional benefics and malefics are derived from moolatrikona lordship, not merely natural beneficence.
- The Most Effective Point of a house is the ascendant degree carried into every house.
- Close conjunctions and aspects near the MEP are stronger; the Most Malefic Planet is a principal afflictor.
- Planetary strength considers sign placement, exact degree, combustion, infancy/old age and affliction.
- Non-moolatrikona houses are often read through their occupants, significators and dispositors rather than an independent lordship claim.
- Period lords deliver their own significations and the condition of houses they influence; transit results become material when they interact with the operating period.
- Exchanges, badhaka considerations, kendradhipati claims, yogas and divisional charts are subordinated to the system's strength and affliction rules.

### Applied subjects

- Identifying the hidden or principal query.
- Professional difficulties, employment and new business.
- Property purchase and obstruction.
- Health, onset and recovery.
- Marriage and divorce.
- Missing persons.
- Progeny, loss, accidents, hostile relationships, legal complications, arrest, addiction, neurological problems, foreign travel and the health of relatives.
- Remedies strengthen weak functional benefics and protect against functional malefic affliction according to the authors' framework.

## Chappanna or Prasana Sastra

### Form and method

- Fifty-six principal stanzas with editorial notes and comparisons to other classical authorities.
- Begins from the rising sign, its lord, occupants, aspects, dignity, Navamsa and relevant houses.
- Classifies the unspoken subject as Dhatu, Moola or Jeeva: mineral/inanimate, vegetable/rooted, or living/animal.
- In odd signs the Navamsa sequence repeats Dhatu–Moola–Jeeva; in even signs it reverses. The notes add alternative tests based on own Navamsa and planets influencing Lagna or its trines.
- Daivajna Vallabha Chapter X verses 12–13 provide an independent classifier from rising-sign groups plus Sun/Mars, Mercury/Saturn, or Moon/Jupiter/Venus influence. The engine preserves its strict matches, influence-only candidates and mixed-family clause separately from Chappanna's Navamsa sequence.
- Chapter X verses 6–8 also supply a complete sign-length table and seven planet color/shape pairs. The local own/friendly/neutral/inimical Navamsa hierarchy uses the explicit *Prasna Marga* friendship table. The engine selects only a unique highest tier and retains tied leaders without replacing the missing tie-break with generic Shadbala.
- Chapter X verses 3–4 use the same local strongest planet to classify the silent query's subject from its D1 house. The engine retains all stated mappings, explicit unmapped houses, and the source's unresolved house-6 “sister or enemy” grammar.
- Planetary strength includes own/friendly/exalted sign, moolatrikona, Hora, Drekkana and Navamsa.

### Question families

- Success or failure of an undertaking.
- Lost property: recovery, thief or relation, hiding place and direction.
- Journeys, travellers, return, delay, bondage, injury, honour, disease and welfare abroad.
- Enemies, invasion, local versus opposing party, retreat, victory, compromise and peace.
- Gain, loss, land acquisition and benefit through women.
- Illness and recovery.
- Pregnancy, sex of a child and marriage.
- Rain and agricultural conditions.
- Age, sex and nature of persons; persons represented by houses.
- Sexual union, change of country and prosperity/adversity.

### Drekkana appendix

- Describes all thirty-six Drekkanas as visual-symbolic figures.
- These images support descriptions of persons, environments, objects and circumstances.
- OCR text alone is insufficient where the prediction depends on exact iconography or chart placement.

## Sri Neelakanta's Prasna Tantra

### Query doctrine

- Discusses the uses and limits of horary, qualifications of astrologer and querist, formulation of a question and handling multiple questions.
- Uses planetary avasthas and planetary indications to infer condition and subject.
- Maps questions to houses and then evaluates the querent lord, event lord, Moon and relevant occupants/aspects.

### Bhava and special Prasna

- General success, obstruction and malefic combinations.
- Questions of all twelve houses and employer–employee relations.
- Exile and return; missing or travelling persons; alive/dead indications.
- Fortresses, imprisonment and release.
- Illness, recovery, treatment and alleged deity-related affliction within the text's traditional worldview.
- Master and servant.
- Lost property, thief, location and recovery.
- Children, meals, dreams, sports, disputes, ships and rumours.
- Purchase and sale, crops, money, thought, sexuality, trade and future prospects.

### Tajaka relationship logic

- **Ithasala:** an applying relationship in which the faster significator approaches the slower one; normally joins querent and desired matter.
- **Easarapha:** separation rather than application; commonly indicates a matter moving away, breaking or having passed.
- **Nakta:** an intermediary planet transfers influence when the principal significators do not directly connect.
- **Yamaya:** a third planet receives/transfers the influence of two relevant planets under the stated motion conditions.
- **Kamboola:** Ithasala between two principal planets with the Moon also involved; quality varies with the strength of the three planets.
- Results are modified by dignity, combustion, retrogression, house condition, benefic/malefic interference and whether an aspect is applying or separating.
- Worked examples cover wealth, mother, children, absent persons, illness, marriage, strikes, stolen property, lawsuits, foreign travel and profession.

## Umang Taneja's Prashna Nadi Astrology

### Chart construction and significations

- Prefers one genuine “burning question,” with at most a closely related follow-up read from the same chart.
- Uses a random seed from 1 to 249 to determine the horary ascendant; the source also records time and place.
- Rotates houses for relatives before judging their issue.
- Gives primary importance to the cuspal sub-lord and to the houses signified at planet, Nakshatra and sub-lord levels.
- Nodes act through their placement, star connections, conjunctions and represented planets under the book's tables.
- Repeated house significations strengthen a result; mixtures of favourable and adverse houses require weighting rather than simple counting.

### Promise and timing

- Topic-specific house combinations define success, obstruction, loss, separation, recovery or fulfilment.
- Dasa-Bhukti-Antara planets must signify the event houses for an event to materialise.
- Transits activate the promised DBA pattern. Slow planets establish the period; faster planets help precipitate the specific date.
- Natural benefic/malefic character describes quality and circumstances, while house signification decides event relevance.

### Available chapters

- Education, examinations, admission and occult study.
- Litigation, bail, settlement, kidnapping and return.
- Property and vehicle purchase, loan, rental income, loss, partition, sale, residence change, gifts, colour and theft.
- Travel, foreign travel, delay and non-return.
- Career and finance: employment, interviews, change, business, breaks, suspension, promotion, transfer, partnership, recovery of money/property and shares.
- Marriage, love, sexual relationships, divorce and matching.
- The source stops during Chapter 7. Any supposed rules from Chapters 8–11 must not be attributed to this file.

## Reliability rules for future answers

- Always state which book or system supplies a rule.
- Do not combine house combinations from Prashna Nadi with Systems' Approach functional lordship as if they were one doctrine.
- Distinguish natural from functional beneficence.
- Distinguish natal-chart promises from horary-chart judgment when a case study uses both.
- Verify every exact degree, date, period balance and chart placement against the source document.
- Verify Sanskrit verses against the scanned page; the Prasna Tantra OCR contains severe script corruption.
- Verify Chappanna chart geometry and Drekkana imagery visually; many items survive only as image placeholders.
- Never infer missing Umang Taneja chapters from its contents page.
- Treat medical, legal and financial claims as traditional astrological doctrine, not as professional or scientific advice.
- When testimonies conflict, report the conflict and the priority rule used rather than hiding it.
