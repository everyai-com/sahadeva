# Prashna Engine: Missing Sources and Data

This acquisition list is ordered by what most materially improves Sahadeva's Prashna engine. Obtain lawful complete editions and preserve edition, translator, ISBN, pagination and image assets. A book entering the folder is not automatically a publishable rule source: rules still require precise locators, conflict review and worked-example validation.

2. **Prashna Astrology and Remedies, Volumes II–III — M. K. Viswanath Nair.** Volume I is installed as a complete 261-page PDF. Scribd search did not locate Volume II. Volume III was located but the trial download quota was exhausted before a local file was obtained.

## Priority 1: foundational gaps

1. **Bhuvana Deepika (Bhuvana Dipaka) — Padmaprabha Suri; English edition associated with Iranganti Rangacharya.** Compact classical source organized into 36 doorways, including journeys, disease, theft, purchase/sale, pregnancy and disputes. A discovered mirror returned an HTML wrapper rather than a valid PDF, so this remains missing.
2. **Tajika Nilakanthi — Nilakantha Daivajna.** Needed to version the Tajaka aspect/orb and yoga mechanics used by Prasna Tantra instead of inferring them from examples.

Acquired on 2026-09-05: **Prasna Marga Parts I–II** and **Horary Astrology / KP Reader VI**, with preserved PDFs and Firecrawl Markdown derivatives. They are no longer acquisition gaps; rule extraction and validation remain outstanding.

## Priority 2: independent classical witnesses

1. **Daivajna Vallabha — attributed to Varahamihira.** A 31-page English text PDF and Firecrawl Markdown derivative are installed. A critical edition and authorship/translation verification remain desirable.
2. **Prasna Jnana — Bhattotpala.** A source explicitly drawn upon in the Prasna Tantra tradition.

4. **Shatpanchashika — Prithuyasas.** Determine edition overlap before acquisition: Chappanna is a 56-stanza work and may substantially represent this textual family, so this is valuable primarily for a cleaner critical translation.
5. **Prasna Chintamani / Prasna Dipaka / Prasna Pradipa.** Obtain identifiable editions with reliable authorship and pagination; do not merge similarly named works without bibliographic verification.

## Acquisition rule

Prefer publisher copies, licensed ebooks, libraries, public-domain scans or user-owned files. Do not use previews as complete books and do not expose copyrighted source text through public APIs. Store the original, an AI-readable derivative, images/tables, checksum, bibliographic metadata and an OCR-quality report together.
