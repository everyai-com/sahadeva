# Sahadeva - first 100 users plan

Stage: early, free product, low budget. Math: directories 30 + registries/
SEO 20 + Reddit/X 30 + launch/communities 20. All tactics below are free
except mcp.so (paid) and optional small ad tests.

## 1. Directory listings (target: 30 users) - WHY: highest intent, users
search inside ChatGPT/Claude/Muse for "horoscope/panchang".
- Start: submit Tier 1 (DISTRIBUTION.md); prompts optimized for "today's
  panchang", "my dasha", "rashifal" discovery queries.
- Outcome: listing live in 3 directories; Dots + proactive discovery free.
- Needs: 1 day for packets (done) + human submit clicks + review waits.

## 2. Free-tool SEO pages (target: 10 users + compounding) - WHY: "today
panchang / rahu kalam" queries repeat daily with weak AI competition.
- Start: publish one page per high-intent query (today panchang, rahu kalam
  today, vimshottari calculator) linking to the connectors; per-sign daily
  pages later (programmatic SEO).
- Outcome: top-10 for 3+ easy keywords in 60-90 days.
- Needs: small static site; 1 page/day habit.

## 3. Reddit + astro communities (target: 30 users) - WHY: dense Vedic
audience, demo-able in one GIF, "free no account" wins.
- Start: r/vedicastrology + r/hinduism launch posts (video demo, honest
  approximate-labels angle); answer 5 panchang questions/week with the tool.
- Outcome: 500+ upvotes equivalents, 30+ trial users, feedback loop.
- Needs: 3 hrs/week; respect promo rules (value first).

## 4. X launch + MCP community (target: 15 users) - WHY: developers are the
registry audience and amplify to their users.
- Start: launch thread with GIF; post endpoint in MCP Discord; reply to
  VedAstro/Navamsha threads with honest comparison (listed vs BYO-URL).
- Outcome: 50+ repo stars, Smithery installs start ticking.
- Needs: 2 hrs launch + 30 min/week.

## 5. Product Hunt + launch spike (target: 15 users) - WHY: concentrated
burst + backlinks once the ChatGPT listing is live (proof > promise).
- Start: schedule after first directory approval; astrology-meets-AI angle;
  line up 20 supporters from steps 3-4 beforehand.
- Outcome: top-10 day, 200+ site visits, backlink + press pickup chance.
- Needs: 1 day prep + launch-day availability.

## Rules
- No paid ads until a free channel shows conversion; then put $100 behind
  the winner (likely Google high-intent: "free kundli online").
- Weekly review: update tracking/products.csv counts; kill tactics at 0
  after 2 weeks; double down on whatever brings users 1-10 (they define
  the channel mix for products 2-10).
- Every rejection/complaint upgrades the template (rules/RULES.md), so
  growth compounds across the factory, not just Sahadeva.
