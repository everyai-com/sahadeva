# Sahadeva - volume + competition (researched Oct 1, 2026)

## Demand: massive and daily-habit
- AstroSage: 80M+ downloads, ~1.5M DAU, ~80% share of kundli creation,
  growing ~20%/month; ~₹60cr/yr revenue doubling yearly since 2020.
- AstroTalk: FY25 ~₹1,214cr revenue, 50M+ downloads (human-astrologer calls).
- Co-Star (US): 20M+ downloads, ~$400K/month; Nebula ~3.9M downloads/yr.
- High-intent keyword clusters: daily horoscope / aaj ka rashifal, free
  kundli by date of birth, today panchang, muhurat 2026, kundli matching,
  Rahu kalam today. Daily-repeat queries = perfect for Dots background jobs
  ("every morning tell me my rashifal + rahu kalam").

## Competition inside AI directories: thin
- VedAstro MCP (mcp.vedastro.org) and Navamsha (FastMCP hosted, Swiss
  Ephemeris + Lahiri) exist as BYO-MCP endpoints, plus OpenEphemeris with a
  ChatGPT install path. None found as proper reviewed listings across all
  three directories with prompts, skills, and Dots-ready design.
- Our edge: listed in ChatGPT + Claude + Muse directories (not a paste-in
  URL), example prompts for discovery, all-reads Dots-ready, panchang +
  dasha + muhurta in one server, honest accuracy labels.

## Positioning
"Sahadeva - Vedic panchang, kundli summary, Vimshottari dasha and daily
rashifal." Free, no account. Later: paid detailed reports via Stripe
(Muse) once traction proves out. Accuracy: low-precision ephemeris
(approx Sun/Moon, Lahiri ayanamsa), labeled approximate - upgrade path to
Swiss Ephemeris port if reviews demand it.

## Risks
- Claude compliance: no AI-media issue here; predictions are interpretive
  text (allowed). Keep health/finance predictions out; add entertainment
  disclaimer in listing copy.
- Accuracy complaints at nakshatra/pada edges (Moon parallax ~1 deg).
  Mitigation: honest labels + "verify critical muhurta" note.
