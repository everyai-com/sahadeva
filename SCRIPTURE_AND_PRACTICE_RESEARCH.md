# Scripture and practice research program

## Epistemic standard

Sahadeva must never simulate seniority by declaring itself a thirty-year astrologer. Experience is represented through reviewed evidence: textual variants, named commentaries, worked examples, practitioner decisions, counterexamples, edge-case tests and revision history.

No text is considered "understood" merely because it was embedded or summarized by a model. A passage becomes usable only after transcription, translation review, rule formalization, worked-example validation and approval by qualified practitioners from the relevant tradition.

## Canon map

### Astronomical and calendar foundation

- Surya Siddhanta: traditional astronomical concepts, calendrical units and planetary models.
- Aryabhatiya: time, mean motions and mathematical astronomy.
- Siddhanta Shiromani: mathematical and astronomical commentary.
- Regional drik and vakya panchanga traditions.

Traditional astronomy is studied historically. Modern astronomical coordinates remain independently validated against observational reference data.

### Parashari natal practice

- Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, with manuscript and edition differences recorded.
- Brihat Jataka.
- Saravali.
- Phaladeepika.
- Jataka Parijata.
- Uttara Kalamrita.
- Hora Sara.

These sources cover grahas, rashis, bhavas, lordship, aspects, dignity, yogas, dashas, strengths and divisional charts. Rules from different editions must not be silently merged.

### Jaimini practice

- Jaimini Upadesa Sutras.
- Major traditional and modern commentaries, stored as separate interpretive layers.

Chara karakas, rashi drishti, arudha, argala and Jaimini dashas require an explicitly selected school because calculation and interpretive variants are material.

### Prashna and Kerala tradition

- Prashna Marga.
- Krishneeyam and relevant Kerala commentarial practice where rights and reliable editions permit.

Prashna cannot be reduced to natal rules. Query time, omens, arudha, purity of question, planetary state and contextual judgment form a distinct workflow.

### Muhurta and panchanga

- Muhurta Chintamani.
- Kalaprakasika.
- Dharmasindhu and Nirnayasindhu for observance rules where applicable.
- Regional Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam panchanga manuals.

Festival and muhurta rules are location, sunrise, school and community sensitive. Each published result needs a named preset.

### Marriage compatibility

- Classical kuta material plus regional porutham manuals.
- Tamil ten-porutham practice.
- Kerala matching practice, including regional weighting and exception handling.
- Telugu and Kannada variants.

The system must return individual agreements, objections, cancellations and uncertainty. A single compatibility score is insufficient.

### Predictive practice

- Vimshottari and other dasha rules.
- Gochara, ashtakavarga and divisional-chart synthesis.
- Annual techniques only after their separate textual basis is reviewed.
- KP and Nadi methods as separately labeled later traditions, never blended into Parashari output by default.

## How a senior astrologer reasons

The intended reasoning pipeline is not a list of isolated placements:

1. Verify the civil birth record, timezone, location and uncertainty.
2. Declare ayanamsa, node mode, house model and dasha year convention.
3. Assess Lagna, Lagna lord, Moon and Sun before detailed claims.
4. Establish functional lordships for the Lagna.
5. Examine dignity, house placement, conjunction, aspects, combustion and retrogression.
6. Compare promises across D1 and the relevant varga, especially D9.
7. Check strength and cancellation before declaring a yoga or dosha.
8. Separate natal promise from timing.
9. Use dasha, antardasha and transit concurrence for timing.
10. Seek multiple independent confirmations for important claims.
11. Check contrary indications and boundary sensitivity.
12. Calibrate language to birth-time certainty and textual disagreement.
13. Avoid deterministic fear, medical claims and costly remedies.
14. Record why the judgment was made and what could falsify it.

## Edge-case catalog

- Birth time near midnight, sunrise or a timezone transition.
- Historical Indian local mean time and pre-standard-time records.
- Unknown seconds and rounded hospital records.
- Lagna, Moon or planet within an uncertainty interval of a boundary.
- Gandanta and varga boundaries.
- Retrograde station, combustion boundary and planetary war.
- Mean versus true lunar nodes.
- High latitude sunrise and house behavior.
- Repeated or skipped tithi and nakshatra at sunrise.
- Adhika and kshaya lunar months.
- Dasha year-length and inclusive-date conventions.
- Different ayanamsas changing nakshatra, pada or varga.
- Conflicting yoga definitions and cancellation rules.
- Porutham exceptions, Rajju disagreements and dasha sandhi.
- Adoption, uncertain parentage and nontraditional family structures.
- Questions involving health, death, pregnancy, finance or legal outcomes.

## Knowledge record

Every usable rule requires:

- Canonical Sanskrit or regional-language text when legally available.
- Transliteration and literal translation.
- One or more interpretive translations.
- Work, chapter, verse and edition metadata.
- Tradition and commentary lineage.
- Deterministic condition expression.
- Exceptions and contradictory authorities.
- At least one worked example and one counterexample.
- Boundary tests.
- Two independent reviewers for high-impact rules.
- Publication status and revision history.

## Research workflow on Cloudflare

1. Rights-cleared scans and transcriptions are stored in R2.
2. D1 records bibliographic metadata, text variants and review state.
3. Workers AI assists OCR cleanup and draft translation but cannot approve text.
4. Workflows route each passage through translation and practitioner review.
5. Deterministic rule expressions are tested against worked examples.
6. Approved passages are embedded into Vectorize for discovery.
7. Retrieval returns citations; rule execution remains outside vector search.
8. Corrections create new versions rather than rewriting history.

## Accuracy definition

"Super accurate" has four separate meanings and must be measured separately:

- Astronomical accuracy: coordinate and event-time error against reference vectors.
- Derivation accuracy: correct rashi, nakshatra, varga, dasha and panchanga transformations.
- Textual fidelity: interpretations accurately represent the cited source and its exceptions.
- Predictive validity: claims require prospective, consented evaluation and must not be presented as established science.

The first three can be engineered and audited. The fourth must be studied honestly rather than asserted.
