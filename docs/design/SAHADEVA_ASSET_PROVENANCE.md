# Sahadeva asset provenance and cultural review ledger

## Status statement

The Sahadeva asset system is **design-compliant and research-informed**. It is not yet culturally approved asset by asset. The visual forms are contemporary interface interpretations; they are not claimed to be recovered historical symbols unless a future ledger entry supplies direct evidence for that claim.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Primary or classical text, with passage-level citation where the concept is textual.
2. Government or institutional astronomical standard for calculated calendric concepts.
3. Qualified Jyotiṣa, Sanskrit, and cultural review for meaning, recognizability, and respectful representation.
4. Sahadeva design rules for geometry, accessibility, motion, responsive behavior, and consumer explanation.

Design rules may control expression, but they may not create a traditional claim.

## Approval record required per asset

Each production-approved asset must eventually record:

- stable asset ID and version;
- Sanskrit name in Devanāgarī, IAST transliteration, and supported regional-language forms;
- literal meaning separated from interpretive meaning;
- source ID, edition, chapter/verse or institutional calculation definition;
- explanation of the chosen visual metaphor;
- explicit classification: historical form, research reconstruction, or modern interpretation;
- reviewer name, expertise, decision, date, and requested corrections;
- 20px recognition, monochrome, contrast, screen-reader, and reduced-motion results.

Until those fields are complete, the registry status remains `pending-qualified-human-review`.
