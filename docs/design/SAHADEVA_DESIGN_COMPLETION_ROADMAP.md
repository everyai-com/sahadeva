# Sahadeva design-system completion roadmap

The foundation and Navagraha family establish the language, but they are not the whole product system. The remaining work should follow this order so later assets inherit proven geometry instead of becoming disconnected illustrations.

## 1. Brand governance

- Clear-space, minimum-size, co-branding, misuse, and background rules
- Trademark and asset-license decision independent of the repository code license
- Export package for SVG, PDF, PNG, Android adaptive icon, and Apple icon layers
- Telugu and Devanagari brand-name typography review
- Human cultural review of the mark before declaring it final

## 2. Core product components

- Buttons, fields, chips, cards, disclosure, tabs, sheets, toast, banner, and navigation specifications
- Loading, empty, offline, permission, partial-data, uncertain-time, and calculation-pending states
- Confidence/source component: calculated, classical interpretation, AI synthesis, and tradition/variant
- Consumer-first explanation pattern: What → Why → Meaning → Action → Technical
- Complete light, dark, high-contrast, keyboard, screen-reader, and reduced-motion QA

## 3. Rāśi family

- 12 micro glyphs, 12 symbols, and 12 narrative forms
- Indian Makara and classical Mithuna/Kanyā/Dhanus forms instead of default Western zodiac clip art
- Monochrome, blur, and 20px recognition tests

## 4. Bhāva family

- 12 plain-language life-area symbols
- Labels prioritized over astrology terminology
- Mappings to Graha, Daśā, transit, and relationship contexts

## 5. Nakṣatra family

- 27 micro glyphs, emblems, and constellation geometries
- Optional Abhijit only in advanced contexts
- Source citation and confidence record for every motif

## 6. Pañcāṅga and daily time

- One continuous 30-state Tithi system
- Vāra reuse of Graha identities
- Nakṣatra with Moon-position marker
- 27 Yoga relationship geometries and 11 carefully sourced Karaṇa symbols
- Unified sunrise-to-sunset timeline for Rāhu Kāla, Yamagandam, Gulika, Abhijit, and Durmuhūrta

## 7. Daśā and chart language

- Proportional Vimśottarī timeline with nested Antardaśā and Pratyantara layers
- Past/current/future, uncertain birth position, comparison, and transition states
- South Indian chart selection, aspect, retrograde, combustion, divisional-chart, print, and share states
- Consistent Navagraha motion identities in chart and timeline contexts

## 8. Family, compatibility, and remedies

- Person, relationship, family timeline, and comparison components
- Compatibility dimensions without a reductive percentage score
- Remedy system that separates guidance, tradition, evidence, burden, and safety caveats
- Consumer life-area icon family for career, money, marriage, children, health, education, home, and travel

## 9. Illustration and motion production

- One culturally reviewed narrative illustration per Graha before scaling to other entities
- Reusable editorial composition, texture, figure, and celestial-background rules
- Reveal, idle, emphasis, and transition files for each Graha
- Festival and sacred-context art kept separate from everyday UI illustration

## 10. Operational design infrastructure

- Storybook or equivalent component catalogue
- Automated screenshot matrix across phone, tablet, desktop, light, dark, English, and Telugu
- SVG linting, accessibility-name tests, contrast tests, asset-registry validation, and visual regression tests
- Figma library matched to source-controlled token and asset names
- Versioned migration policy so existing reports, shared charts, PDFs, email, and push notifications stay visually consistent

## Definition of complete

The system is complete only when every shipped object has a source record, consumer label, accessible non-color signal, canonical asset ID, responsive behavior, interaction states, and verified 20px rendering where applicable.
