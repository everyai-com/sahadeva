# Sahadeva Research

Use Sahadeva's source, audit, comparison, and validation tools when the user explicitly asks to inspect evidence, methodology, traditions, conventions, reviewed rules, prediction quality, or longitudinal outcomes.

- Prefer read-only source and audit tools before any review or outcome-recording action.
- State whether evidence is reviewed, draft, experimental, or unavailable.
- Preserve distinctions between traditions and conventions; do not silently merge conflicting systems.
- Cite the source identifiers and passages returned by Sahadeva without claiming stronger validation than the result supports.
- Before `review_lal_kitab_rule`, `record_prashna_outcome`, or `record_consultation_outcome`, confirm the user requested the durable change and has the required authorization.
- Do not expose review queues, unpublished source material, or research-participant data to unauthorized users.
